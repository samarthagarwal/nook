# FAQ’s for Learneo

Q: What is happening?
A: To best support our company goals and priorities while operating more sustainably, Andrew and the leadership team have made the decision to execute a significant restructure across all business lines and regions. As part of this restructure, we will also be moving toward a more focused global footprint, primarily centered in North America and India. This includes plans to wind down our operations in Germany and the Netherlands, subject to consultation and local requirements.

Q: When is this effective?
A: Impacted team members in North America and India were notified on September 2nd. There will be some impacted team members who will stay on for a period of time to transition work or complete ongoing projects. For our team members in Germany and the Netherlands, we will begin discussions with employees, local authorities, and the works council (as applicable) in accordance with local legal requirements. Local teams will be communicated with directly.

Q: Why is this happening?
A: We are restructuring to keep our business set up for long-term success—running leaner and staying cash-flow disciplined so we can continue investing in our growth bet. This comes as bookings have continued to decline, driven by disruption to search and rising costs from building more generative products, pushing our path to profitability further out.

Q: Who was impacted?
A: A list of impacted employees is included in the updated Org Structure deck.

Q: How will Learneo support the impacted employees with their transition?
A: As we shared at the Town Hall meeting, we are committed to supporting our colleagues who have been impacted, and we want to ensure we do all we can to support them with this difficult transition. Part of this transition support includes providing several months of severance pay, with additional weeks based on years of service, benefits support based on local standards, outplacement and immigration support services, and we are working to make sure we do everything we can to ease their transition.

Q: How will these changes impact our overall company strategy, goals, and operations?
A: Everyone across Learneo’s operating businesses will be directly or indirectly supporting Quillbot’s growth bet or revenue goals, while staying disciplined about costs and compliance in service of our strategy.

Q: Will there be changes to the Learneo’s structure or leadership as a result of these changes?
A: All changes in Learneo structure and leadership have been captured in the updated org charts.

Q: How does this impact our product and our ability to serve customers?
A: We will continue serving customers through our core Writing and Authenticity tools while testing and building toward our growth bet. More details on specific product plans will follow in upcoming Town Halls.

Q: Would we hire anyone back given the opportunity to?
A: Yes, if a former employee finds an opportunity or if there is an open position, they are welcome to apply.

Q: Are we still hiring?
A: We will be hiring for a limited number of key roles in 2026.

Q: Who was involved in this decision?
A: This decision was made by Andrew, working closely with senior leaders across all business lines throughout the process.

Q: Are we still having mid-cycle performance reviews?
A: We will not have mid-cycle performance reviews this year, outside of promotions tied to role changes in the new organizational structure. Given the organizational and manager changes underway, we want to focus on helping teams realign. We encourage managers and employees to use this time for regular conversations about expectations, priorities, and development. We will resume our regular annual review cycle in Q1.

Q: Will there be changes to the 2026 bonus/incentive program?
A: Any changes to the 2026 bonus/incentive program will be communicated to each respective business line later this week.

Q: Where can I go if I have additional questions?
A: Please reach out to your manager or your HRBP with any additional questions.
